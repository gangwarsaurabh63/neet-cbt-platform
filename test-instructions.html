General Instructions / महत्वपूर्ण निर्देश

  • 1. The test is of 3 hrs 15 mins duration and contains 180 multiple-choice questions from Physics (45 Ques.), Chemistry (45 Ques.), and Biology (90 Ques.). All questions are compulsory.
  • 2. Each question carries 4 marks. For each correct response, you will get +4 marks. For each incorrect response, -1 mark will be deducted from the total score. Maximum marks are 720.
  • 3. The clock will be set at the server. The countdown timer in the top right corner of test screen will display the remaining time available for you to complete the exam.
  • 4. Question Palette displays the status of each question using distinct color codes (Attempted, Unattempted, Bookmarked).
  • 5. Ensure you review your answers before final submission. Once submitted, your scorecard will be generated automatically.
